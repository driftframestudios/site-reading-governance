# Administrative assurance runbooks

Choose the route by the decision that needs evidence. Each route uses the
[assurance companion guide](../../docs/ADMINISTRATIVE_ASSURANCE.md) and preserves
Program's domain meanings and Governance's assurance authority.

| Trigger | Route | Receiving result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A statement concerns a population or subgroup | [Population claim review](population-claim-review.v0.1.md) | Scoped statement, denominator, omissions, review standing |
| A proposed administrative action needs review or correction | [Decision assurance and recourse](administrative-decision-assurance-and-recourse.v0.1.md) | Preserved proposal, owned review, reversible next move |
| Policy, source, purpose, or implementation changes | [Source and claim invalidation](source-and-claim-invalidation.v0.1.md) | Affected claims held in an immutable successor |
| Another repository receives the contract | [Governance contract handoff](governance-contract-handoff.v0.1.md) | Exact source, fitting checks, and one next receiver |

The named exercises use wholly synthetic inputs and modeled roles. Optional
external-source review starts through its own source and execution approval.
