# Historical Control Models

Earlier collaboration cycles generated a broad control-plane experiment. The
experiment included these schema and example families:

- acceptance and approval;
- bounded callers, operations, effects, and call receipts;
- decisions, claims, simulations, comparisons, and invalidations;
- receipts and simulated receipt closure;
- database migration receipts, reconciliation, and release manifests; and
- first-receipt and promotion records.

These files preserve design provenance. They are excluded from the active
example map, root orientation validation, and CI assertions. Application teams
use established identity providers, policy engines, workflow systems, GitHub
Rulesets, Flyway, vendor lifecycle tooling, and organizational systems of record
for the corresponding operational concerns.

The maintained artifacts are listed in the root README. A future collaborator
may extract a historical idea after demonstrating a concrete implementation need
that the selected platform leaves unresolved.
