# Administrative decision assurance and recourse 0.1

## Entry and authority

Receive the proposed statement, Program decision/input, admitted study, and
ruleset identity. Locate raw input and normalized classification at their source
owner. The companion binds catalog, policy, scoring, source, and execution.
A modeled reviewer can exercise the review process; an operational effect stays
with its authenticated service and accountable authority.

## Checks and exercise

1. Keep the proposal distinct from deterministic admission and the eventual
   review decision. Locate matched rules, missing evidence, and policy effectivity
   in the Program/Enterprise decision source.
2. Use the raw-preserving classification example from the complete study.
   Inspect reportability, enrollment, and policy state as separate dimensions.
3. Receive a review-ready output statement, then call `record_review` with its
   claim ID, a distinct successor ID, and a bounded interpretation note.
4. Request review of a held configured-execution statement. Expect `review_entry`
   and retain the prior record unchanged.
5. For recourse, name the source owner, disputed evidence, requested correction,
   and stopping event. Receive the corrected source through its typed owner,
   recompute the report, and prepare a fresh companion before another review.

## Recovery and stopping event

Required evidence in pending standing keeps the action with its review owner.
A caller can narrow the statement or obtain the missing source. Override and
supersession preserve the original proposal, reason, actor role, and successor;
a modeled review grants only the declared exercise standing.

## Handback and acceptance event

Return the original and successor identities, review note, permitted next move,
and correction route. Acceptance of this exercise requires preserved raw values,
separate proposal/validation/review/effect roles, and an inspectable recourse path.
